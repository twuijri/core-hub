The first release of Core Hub on the App Store.

• Sign in to your own Core Hub server with its address, username and password, or by scanning the pairing code it shows on the web
• Chat with your agents: replies stream in, with Markdown, tools, thinking time, approvals and questions
• Attach photos and files, dictate messages and hear replies read aloud
• Chats, tasks and schedules across every profile, with search
• Notifications when a reply is ready or an agent is waiting for you
